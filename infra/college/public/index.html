Cost of attendance (future $)

Model notes. Full COA (tuition, fees, housing, food, books, transport, personal) — no student-earnings offset, so a part-time job stays optional. Costs inflate from the 2024–25 base to each academic year. Contributions run monthly from your review date to matriculation (Aug 2039); the balance keeps growing at ~4% during college, with each year's cost withdrawn up front. "Fully fund upfront" solves for a monthly amount that covers all four years with no help during college; "Blend" lets cash flow during college cover part, lowering the monthly ask. This is a planning estimate, not financial advice — returns aren't guaranteed. All contributions and cash flow are flat nominal dollars (not inflation-adjusted); only the cost of attendance is inflated.
